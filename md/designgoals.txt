ZIA Global — Strategic Proposal Template

A cinematic, scrollable proposal template for presenting a luxury travel brand’s global flagship rebuild.

Key Features
Story-led slide flow: Ten snap-scrolling sections from problem, narrative, and solution through options and execution.
Luxury editorial aesthetic: Dark, quiet-luxury palette, serif typography, film-first imagery, and rounded UI.
Service & options framing: Clear Option A/B packaging with inclusions, pricing bands, and timelines.
Experience-led structure: Highlights destination library, experience collections, and enquiry planner flows.
Moodboard interaction preview: Visual profiling concept for undecided guests.
Primary Use Case
Ideal for travel ateliers or creative agencies to pitch a high-end, global web rebuild and better enquiry experience to clients.

Benefits
Communicates strategy and scope in a polished, on-brand format.
Helps clients visualize core pages, flows, and trust signals.
Reinforces premium positioning with calm, confident storytelling.
